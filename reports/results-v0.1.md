# HumanBench v0.1 research preview: results

Generated by `python -m humanbench.harness.report_v01`. Read `README.md` for what these numbers do and do not mean: they measure task compliance, a minimum quality bar, and detectability under the named open AI-text detectors. They are not a measure of whether people can tell the text from human writing.

## The HumanBench score

One benchmark, one score: **the share of a model's outputs that pass all three gates while doing the job**. An output counts only if it passes the programmatic checks, is accepted by an LLM judge, and is not flagged by the AI-text detector at the threshold that 99% of human-written texts stay under. Three samples are produced per task; a task's score is the share of its three outputs that count, and a model's score is the average over all 212 tasks. Scores are low because nearly every output is flagged.

**132 tasks are public**: 52 built backwards from a real person's text (human-reference tasks) and 80 synthetic tasks. **80 are held out**: synthetic tasks of the same kinds, held back so that no model can be trained or tuned on them. The score on each part is shown beside the overall score: the public part can be reproduced by anyone, and the held-out part shows whether it holds on tasks nobody has seen. The two parts are not expected to be equal, because the public part also contains the human-reference tasks, which are harder.

## Detectors and their thresholds

| detector | human texts scored / in the pool | threshold (higher = more AI) |
|---|---|---|
| Open Pangram large (`editlens-llama-3.2-3b`) (headline) | 801 / 801 | 0.1641 |
| Open Pangram small (`editlens-roberta-large`) | 801 / 801 | 0.1604 |
| vanguard (`vanguard-modernbert-large`) | 801 / 801 | 0.5508 |
| tropa-mini (`tropa-mini-deberta-large`) | 801 / 801 | 3.0628 |

A threshold targets 1% of the human calibration texts flagged. It is a calibration on archival text, not a validated false-positive rate. vanguard and tropa-mini read the whole text with typography and whitespace normalised; Open Pangram small reads the first 512 tokens and Open Pangram large the first 1,024, with Pangram's own preprocessing.

How to read the intervals: they are bootstrap intervals over tasks. An interval of [0.0, 0.0] means no win was observed in this sample, not that the true rate is zero.

## Human baseline for the comparison with the human reference

For the human-reference tasks, the report also gives how often a model's piece is rated as more human than the human reference. To show what a human scores on that scale, other real human texts are put in the model's seat: about half the time they win, so 50% is parity there. They are different pieces, not outputs to the same request, and they come from the same archival pool as the references, so this does not test old text against new text.
